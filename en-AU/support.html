Marutto Tracker Support

About the App

"Marutto Tracker" is a tracking app that supports your daily habit building. The habit fairy "Marutto" watches over your efforts and cheers you on. As you keep up your habits, travel photos will be added to your collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I transfer my data?

A. In the current version, data is stored only on your device and cannot be transferred to other devices. We are considering adding this feature in a future update.

Q. Can I remove ads?

A. The current version does not offer an ad removal feature. We may consider adding this option in a future update.

Q. The app is not working properly

A. Please try the following:

  • Completely close and restart the app
  • Update iOS to the latest version
  • Restart your device

Contact Us

If the above does not resolve your issue, please contact us using the form below.

How do I share a photo and diary?

Open a collected photo and tap Share. You can send the photo, Marutto’s travel diary, the app link, and #MaruttoTracker to the app you choose.

How do I change the language?

Choose Language in Settings. Automatic follows your device’s language preferences.
